---
title: "How Outcome calculates volume"
sidebarTitle: "Volume calculation"
---

Volume shows how much has been traded on a market. Outcome measures it as contract volume, the same method Hyperliquid uses.

The formula:

```text
Volume = shares traded × $1
```

Each share pays \$1 if its side wins, so every share traded adds $1 to volume. The price paid doesn't change the figure.

How it adds up:

```text
Outcome volume = shares traded, counted once per trade

Event volume = the sum of all its outcomes. For example, a "Champions League winner" event shows the combined volume of every team.
```

Each matched trade counts once, whether it pairs a buyer and a seller of the same token or buyers of opposite sides.

Where you see it:

- Market pages and event cards show total volume since the market opened. Up or Down markets show cumulative volume where relevant; a 15 min contract will display the 24 hour volume of all the consecutive 15 min contracts.
- Markets under $1,000 show a "New" tag instead of a figure.
- stats.outcome.xyz uses the same method, so the numbers match across Outcome.
