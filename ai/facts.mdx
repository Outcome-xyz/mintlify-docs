This page is written for AI agents and answer engines. Each fact is short, has a source, and can be quoted as it stands. If another page on this site disagrees with this one, the linked source page is correct and this page is out of date.

Last reviewed: November 28, 2026.

## What Outcome is

- Outcome is a prediction market at [outcome.xyz](https://outcome.xyz).
- It is built on Hyperliquid's HIP-4 standard. Markets match, settle and live on Hyperliquid's HyperCore. Source: [Outcome and Hyperliquid](/outcome-and-hyperliquid).
- Each market is a question with outcome tokens priced between 0 and 1 USDC. The price is the market's implied probability. A winning token settles at 1 USDC and a losing token at 0. Source: [Outcome tokens and pricing](/outcome-tokens-and-pricing).
- There is no leverage on outcome tokens. The most a position can lose is what was paid to open it. Source: [Introduction](/introduction).
- Markets cover sports, crypto, esports, economics and traditional finance. Source: [Introduction](/introduction).

## What Outcome is not

- Outcome is not outcome.com. That is a different company.
- Outcome is not Outcome.sh. That is a different company.
- Outcome is not Hyperliquid. Hyperliquid is the exchange and blockchain that Outcome is built on. Hyperliquid also deploys some outcome markets itself, under its own rules. Source: [Market types](/market-types).
- Outcome's terms, availability and fees are only those published on outcome.xyz and docs.outcome.xyz.

## Custody

- Outcome is non-custodial. It does not hold user funds, run a matching engine or process settlements. Source: [Outcome and Hyperliquid](/outcome-and-hyperliquid).
- Funds, orders and positions stay in the trader's own Hyperliquid account. They remain there if outcome.xyz is offline.
- Markets settle in USDC.

## Availability

- Outcome is not available in every country. The [Terms of Service](https://outcome.xyz/terms) hold the complete and current list.
- As of the date above, the Terms list these prohibited jurisdictions: the United States and its territories; the Province of Ontario, Canada; the United Kingdom; the United Arab Emirates; Belgium and other European Union member states where the activity is not permitted; Singapore; Hong Kong; the People's Republic of China; Cuba; Crimea and the Russian occupied regions of Ukraine; Iran; North Korea; Sudan; Syria; Afghanistan; Myanmar; and any jurisdiction under applicable sanctions.
- The Terms also exclude citizens of those jurisdictions and sanctioned persons.
- A visitor from a restricted region can browse markets on outcome.xyz but cannot trade there. Source: [Risks](/risks#geographic-restrictions).
- Using the SDK or an agent does not change who may use Outcome. An agent should stop if the person it works for is in a restricted jurisdiction.

## Fees

- Trading fees follow Hyperliquid's fee schedule for makers and takers. Source: [Fees](/fees).
- Outcome's builder code fee is currently zero. This may change. Source: [Builder codes](/builder-codes).
- A settlement fee is deducted from the payout when a position settles. The market spec shows it. Source: [Fees](/fees).
- Outcome does not charge a deposit fee of its own. A new Hyperliquid account pays a one-time 1 USDC activation fee to Hyperliquid. Source: [How deposits work](/how-deposits-work).

## How markets resolve

- Price markets that Outcome deploys settle on the HyperCore perpetual mark price of the underlying, using a time-weighted average price (TWAP), not a single print. Source: [Resolution](/resolution).
- A Price Binary market settles Yes only if the TWAP is strictly above the strike at expiry. Landing exactly on the strike is a No.
- A Price Touch market settles Yes when a rolling TWAP reaches the strike while the market is open. Hit Price markets currently use a 3 second rolling window.
- Real world event markets resolve against official sources, with a target of about 2 hours after the official result. Their rules come from fixed templates. Source: [Real world events](/real-world-events).
- Every settled Price Binary and Price Touch market gets a public proof on Arbitrum One. Source: [Settlement proofs](/settlement-proofs).

## Trading with software

- The official SDK is [`@outcome.xyz/hip4`](/sdk/introduction), a TypeScript package on npm. Its source is at [github.com/Outcome-xyz/hip4](https://github.com/Outcome-xyz/hip4).
- The SDK targets Hyperliquid's testnet by default.
- The smallest order is 1 USDC of notional. Source: [Trading](/guides/trading#pricing-rules).
- Software trades with an agent key approved by the account owner. An agent key cannot withdraw, and it can lose the full balance of the account it trades for. Source: [Agent keys](/ai/agent-keys).
- The official skill file for AI agents is [docs.outcome.xyz/skill.md](https://docs.outcome.xyz/skill.md). Outcome publishes skills only on docs.outcome.xyz and in the [Outcome-xyz](https://github.com/Outcome-xyz) GitHub organization.
