---
title: "Withdrawing USDC from Outcome to Arbitrum"
sidebarTitle: "Withdrawing funds"
---

You can withdraw your USDC balance from Outcome at any time. There are two ways out: bridge USDC to another chain, or cash out to fiat.

### Withdraw USDC to Arbitrum

USDC withdrawals are powered by [Circle](https://www.circle.com/)'s [Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP)](https://www.circle.com/cross-chain-transfer-protocol), which moves native USDC across chains. Open the Withdraw flow in [outcome.xyz](http://outcome.xyz), enter the amount, choose the destination chain (currently set as Arbitrum by default) and address, and confirm.

You can send to your connected wallet's address or any other EVM address you specify. Double-check the destination before confirming. Crypto transactions cannot be reversed if you send to the wrong address.

### Withdraw to fiat (coming soon)

If you want to cash out to fiat currency, withdrawals are powered by [Meld](https://meld.io/), a crypto off-ramp aggregator. Meld converts your USDC to fiat and sends it to your chosen payment method. This requires identity verification (KYC). Availability, supported currencies, and payment methods depend on your region.

### Fees and timing

**Fees.** Two fees apply to a USDC withdrawal, both shown before you confirm: a small HyperEVM gas fee (a fraction of a cent) and Circle's CCTP forwarding fee, which varies by destination chain (roughly 20 cents to Arbitrum, more to chains like Ethereum). Fiat withdrawals via Meld incur Meld's fees, which depend on the payment method and region. Outcome charges no withdrawal fee of its own.

**Timing.** Withdrawals typically complete within seconds once submitted.

### Activation fee

The first time a brand-new HyperCore account makes an outbound action, Hyperliquid charges a one-time 1 USDC activation fee. It's charged by the protocol, not by Outcome.

Outcome shows it in the deposit breakdown so you see the full cost up front, but it isn't taken from your deposit — the protocol deducts it on your first withdrawal or transfer.
