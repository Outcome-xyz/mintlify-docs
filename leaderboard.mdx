---
title: "Leaderboard"
---

Outcome has a leaderboard where users can see traders' profit and loss across the platform. It's a way to see how others are doing on Outcome and compare your own trading against the community.

The leaderboard ranks traders by their P&L or volume, with traders identified by their connected wallet or profile.

### Periods

Use the tabs at the top to choose a period: **Today**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **All**. The rows re-rank for the period you pick.

### What each row shows

Each row shows the trader's rank, avatar, username and badges, with their profit and loss in dollars, their profit and loss in percent, and their volume. Switch between the dollar, percent and volume views, and toggle the sort order, to rank traders the way you want.

### Finding a trader

Search by username to find a specific trader. Select a row to open that trader's profile.
