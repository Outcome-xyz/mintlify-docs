Every market on Outcome ends with an answer. Here's how we get to it.

Outcome runs its own resolution system, built with established oracle and resolution partners. And you don't have to take our word for it: every settled Price Binary and Price Touch market gets a public proof on Arbitrum One, recorded by a Chainlink network of 10 nodes. The nodes recalculate Price Binary results and Price Touch Yes results from price data. A Price Touch No is recorded as Outcome's attestation for now. See [Settlement proofs](/settlement-proofs).

For real-world events, the goal during mainnet rollout is to resolve within ~2 hours of the official result from authoritative sources, and to bring that down to minutes over time.

## Price markets

Price markets that Outcome deploys settle on one source: the HyperCore perpetual **<u>mark price</u>** for the market's underlying. Instead of a single price print, they use a **TWAP** (time-weighted average price), so one stray trade can't swing the result. Markets that Hyperliquid deploys and settles, such as its daily Up/Down and Price Range markets, follow Hyperliquid's own rules instead (see [Market types](/market-types)). Want the math? [TWAP calculation](/twap-calculation) walks through it with real numbers.

### Price Binary

Was the price above the strike at expiry? Outcome averages the price over a short window that ends at expiry, and compares that TWAP to the strike. Only the seconds **before** expiry count; nothing after expiry is used. Each contract's rules state its window. For example, the 15 minute Up/Down markets use the 60 seconds right before expiry.

- TWAP **strictly above** the strike: settles **Yes**.
- TWAP **at or below** the strike: settles **No**. Landing exactly on the strike is a No.

### Price Touch

Did the price ever reach the strike while the market was open? Outcome watches a rolling TWAP of the underlying rather than raw prints. Each contract's rules state the rolling window; Hit Price markets currently use 3 seconds. A touch registers when that TWAP trades at or through the strike. Once it does, the contract settles Yes right away and stays Yes, wherever the price goes next. If the TWAP never gets there before expiry, it settles No.

Because the touch uses a TWAP, a quick wick that reverts within a few seconds won't necessarily count. What matters is whether the average across the rolling window clears the strike, not whether any single print did.

### Price Range

Price Range markets, where each outcome is a price bucket, aren't deployed by Outcome. Hyperliquid deploys and settles them under its own rules, so they don't have an Outcome settlement proof. See [Market types](/market-types).

## Mark Price

We use [<u>HyperCore's mark price</u>](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/robust-price-indices), which is the median of three inputs: Hyperliquid's oracle price, its live order book, and a blend of prices from major exchanges such as Binance and OKX. Moving it means moving several independent sources at the same time. HIP-3 assets follow each deployer's own setup, built on similar principles. _(See the deployers docs for details on how mark price is calculated.)_

## Who stands behind it

Outcome has posted a 500K HYPE bond, so getting results right and settling them in good faith is in our own interest.
