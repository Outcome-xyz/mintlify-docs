If you don't hold crypto yet, you can buy USDC with a card or bank transfer without leaving Outcome. The purchase is handled by [Swapped.com](https://swapped.com/), and the USDC lands in your account when it clears.

## Buy USDC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Deposit panel">
    Go to [outcome.xyz](https://outcome.xyz), open **Deposit**, and choose the **Cash** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the purchase">
    Swapped's widget opens inside the app. Choose your currency, payment method, and amount, then follow their checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your identity, if asked">
    First-time purchases usually require identity verification (KYC). Swapped handles this — Outcome never sees your documents.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for it to clear">
    Once the payment settles, USDC arrives in your account. You'll be notified when it lands.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Swapped controls

Swapped owns the entire purchase, so these depend on them rather than on Outcome:

- Which countries and regions are supported
- Which currencies and payment methods are available
- Identity verification requirements
- Their fees, and how long payment takes to clear
- Any purchase limits on your account

Outcome is not affiliated with Swapped and can't intervene in a purchase, alter their limits, or resolve a payment dispute on your behalf. If something goes wrong during checkout, contact Swapped's support.

## Fees and limits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum** | $11 |
| **Fees** | Set by Swapped, shown before you pay |
| **Outcome fee** | None |

<Note>
  Cash deposits take longer than crypto deposits, because a card or bank payment has to clear before the USDC is released. Card payments are usually quickest.
</Note>

Your USDC appears in your account balance, ready to trade — the same place every other deposit method delivers to.
