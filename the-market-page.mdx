Every market on Outcome has its own page. It's where you read the odds, check the order book, place trades, and track your positions and orders in one place. To open it, click a market on the home page or in a category.

### Header

The header shows the market's title and date, a **Rules** button, and headline figures such as the current chance and the market's volume. For how volume is counted, see [How Outcome calculates volume](/outcome-volume-calculation).

**Rules** opens the market's resolution terms, which spell out exactly how the market resolves. See [Resolution](/resolution).

When you scroll down, the header shrinks into a bar that stays at the top of the page, so the title and key figures stay in view.

### Chart

The chart shows how the odds have moved. You can switch between a line chart and candlesticks. On markets that settle against a price, the chart can also show the target price next to the current price, with a countdown to expiry, and you can switch back to the outcome odds.

The **Embed** button opens a dialog where you get code to put the market on your own site. See [Embed markets](/guides/embedded-markets).

### Order book

The order book lists the bids and asks for the outcome you're looking at. See [Reading the order book](/reading-the-order-book) for how to read it.

### Outcomes

A market with several outcomes lists each one with its volume and odds. Use **Show resolved** to list the outcomes that have already resolved; the button then reads **Hide resolved**.

### Trading panel

The trading panel is where you place orders. Pick **Buy** or **Sell**, choose an order type, and enter an amount. Each side shows its odds as a percentage and as a multiplier. See [Placing your first trade](/placing-your-first-trade) and [Order types](/order-types).

On a phone, the page has a single column, with a bar fixed at the bottom that shows each side's multiplier, and the trading panel opens as a sheet.

### Your positions and orders

Tables on the page show your activity across your whole portfolio, with this market's rows first:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Positions** | Your open positions |
| **Open Orders** | Orders resting on the book, which you can cancel |
| **History** | Your filled orders, closes, and settlements |
| **Outcomes** | Your resolved outcomes |

**Close All** and **Cancel All** are available here too.

<Warning>
  Close All and Cancel All act on your whole portfolio, not just this market. A single confirmation submits orders for every open position or cancels every open order. A position closes only when its order fills.
</Warning>

See [Closing a position](/closing-a-position) and [Managing your positions](/managing-your-positions).

### Live scores

When a match is in play, the page shows a live scoreboard. Esports matches also show which game is being played, for example **Live · Game 3 of 3**.

### After a market resolves

Once a market has resolved, its page shows the result. On markets that have a settlement proof, the page links to it. For how settlement works, see [Resolution and settlement](/resolution-and-settlement).
