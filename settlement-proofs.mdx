When an Outcome price market settles, you shouldn't have to take our word for the result. For every settled Price Binary and Price Touch market, a Chainlink network of 10 nodes rechecks the market's rules and price data, recalculates the result, and records a signed proof of it on Arbitrum One. Anyone can look that proof up.

This page explains what a settlement proof is, how it is produced, and how to check one. It covers Outcome's use of CRE. To learn about CRE itself, see the [Chainlink CRE documentation](https://docs.chain.link/cre/overview).

## What a settlement proof gives you

- **A separate recheck.** The result is recalculated by Chainlink nodes from the market's published rules and recorded price data. Outcome does not supply the answer.
- **Agreement before anything is recorded.** A proof is written only when at least 4 of the 10 nodes return the exact same result. If they don't, no proof is written.
- **A permanent public record.** Each proof lives on Arbitrum One, a public blockchain. Once stored, it can't be edited, replaced, or deleted.
- **One proof per market.** The proof is tied to the market's exact ID. A second proof for the same market is rejected.

## What Chainlink CRE is

The [Chainlink Runtime Environment (CRE)](https://docs.chain.link/cre/key-terms#chainlink-runtime-environment-cre) runs programs called [workflows](https://docs.chain.link/cre/key-terms#workflow) on a [Decentralized Oracle Network (DON)](https://docs.chain.link/cre/key-terms#decentralized-oracle-network-don), a group of Chainlink nodes. Every node runs the same workflow code and fetches its own data, then the network combines their results before anything is signed. Read more in Chainlink's [consensus computing](https://docs.chain.link/cre/concepts/consensus-computing) guide.

Outcome's workflow runs on a DON of 10 nodes. A result is accepted only when at least 4 nodes return the exact same result and no other result reaches 4. That means the answer doesn't rest on one server: one node with bad data or a bug can't produce a proof on its own.

Outcome runs one CRE workflow for price markets. It is started by an [HTTP trigger](https://docs.chain.link/cre/guides/workflow/using-triggers/http-trigger/overview) when a market settles and writes its result to the blockchain using CRE's [onchain write](https://docs.chain.link/cre/guides/workflow/using-evm-client/onchain-write/overview) capability.

## How a proof is produced

Settlement and the proof are two separate, parallel processes. Outcome settles the market on Hyperliquid through HIP-4, as described in [Resolution and settlement](/resolution-and-settlement). At the same time, Outcome asks the CRE workflow to check that market. The proof never delays, changes, or blocks your payout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The market settles">
    Outcome submits the market's settlement on Hyperliquid. At the same time, it sends the CRE workflow one signed request that contains only the market's ID. Outcome doesn't send a price, a result, or any other input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The nodes verify the market">
    Each node looks up the settled market and checks that it is a genuine Outcome market: it was deployed by an approved Outcome deployer, and it uses a reviewed, approved market template. The strike, dates, and calculation rules all come from that verified market record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The nodes recalculate the result">
    Each node fetches the recorded HyperCore mark prices for the market's window and recalculates the result with the same rules described in [Resolution](/resolution). For Price Binary, that is the TWAP over the seconds right before expiry compared to the strike. For Price Touch, that is whether the rolling TWAP reached the target before the deadline. See [TWAP calculation](/twap-calculation) for the formula and an example of the exact data each node collects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The nodes must agree">
    The network compares the nodes' results. A result is accepted only when at least 4 of the 10 nodes return exactly the same answer and no other answer reaches 4. If the data isn't ready yet, a source is unavailable, or the nodes disagree, nothing is written and the check runs again later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The proof is recorded">
    The network signs a report, and Chainlink delivers it to Outcome's proof contract on Arbitrum One. The contract only accepts reports from Outcome's approved CRE workflow, and only one per market.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Settlement data usually takes a few minutes to become available after a market settles, so a proof typically appears a few minutes later.

## What's in a proof

Each proof records:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Market ID | The HIP-4 ID of the settled market. |
| Market type | Price Binary or Price Touch. |
| Result | Yes or No. |
| Settlement price | For Price Binary, the exact calculated TWAP. For Price Touch, the target price. |
| Settlement time | When the price used for the result was observed. |
| Recorded at | When the proof was written to Arbitrum One. |
| Rules fingerprint | A hash of the market's rules, so the rules can't be swapped after the fact. |
| Evidence fingerprint | A hash of the exact market record and price data the nodes used, so anyone with the same data can reproduce the result. |
| Workflow ID | The CRE workflow that produced the proof. |

## Check a proof yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a settled price market">
    Go to a settled Price Binary or Price Touch market on [outcome.xyz](https://outcome.xyz).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the resolution source">
    In the market's rules, select **Resolution Source**. On mobile, it is in the rules links. This opens the market's proof on the [Outcome proof explorer](https://proof-explorer.outcome.xyz).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the result">
    The proof explorer shows the proof recorded for that market. Check that its result matches the result shown on the market page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Because the proof is stored on a public blockchain, you don't have to rely on the proof explorer. You can read it directly from the proof contract on [Arbiscan](https://arbiscan.io), the Arbitrum One block explorer, using the market's ID.

## Which markets have proofs

Settlement proofs cover two price market types deployed by Outcome: **Price Binary** and **Price Touch**. Other price market types, such as Price Scalar and Price Range, and markets that settle on real-world events, such as sports or economic data, don't have CRE proofs yet. See [Real-world events](/real-world-events) for how those resolve.

The **Resolution Source** link appears once a supported market has ended. If a market has just settled, its proof can take a few minutes to appear.

## What a proof does and doesn't do

- **It confirms the result; it doesn't decide it.** Settlement on Hyperliquid is what pays out your position. The proof is a separate record that the result matches the market's rules.
- **It checks the calculation, not the price feed.** All nodes read the same recorded HyperCore mark prices through the same data provider. The proof shows that the result was calculated correctly from that data, not that the data came from several independent price sources.
- **No proof doesn't mean a wrong result.** If the workflow can't confirm a market, for example because data isn't available yet, it writes nothing rather than guess. Your settlement isn't affected.
