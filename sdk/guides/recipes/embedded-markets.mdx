The card shows the market's odds chart, live trades and buy buttons, and the odds update on their own. A click on the card or a buy button opens the market on outcome.xyz in a new tab. You need no SDK, no wallet and no backend.

## Embed a market

On [outcome.xyz](https://outcome.xyz), open the market and click the embed button (`</>`) under the chart. Pick the options and copy the code.

<Frame>
  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline controls className="w-full h-auto" width="1600" height="1000" poster="/images/recipes/embed-get-code-poster.webp">
    <source src="/images/recipes/embed-get-code.webm" type='video/webm; codecs="av01.0.08M.08"' />
  </video>
</Frame>

Or write the iframe yourself:

```html
<iframe
  title="2026/2027 English Premier League winner on Outcome"
  src="https://outcome.xyz/embed/1473"
  width="400"
  height="300"
  style="width:100%;max-width:400px;height:300px;border:0"
></iframe>
```

`1473` is the market's outcome id. Here it's Arsenal, one option of the English Premier League winner question. For a question, any option's id works, and the card shows the whole question. The id is in the market's URL on outcome.xyz: `/o{id}` for a single market, or `?outcome={id}` on a question page.

Every option is on by default. To turn one off, add it to the `src` URL with `0`, for example `?chart=0&buy=0`.

| Parameter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `chart` | The odds chart |
| `grid` | The chart's grid |
| `yaxis` | The chart's price labels |
| `activity` | Live trades over the chart |
| `buy` | Buy buttons. A question shows its options instead. |

The card fills the iframe, so the iframe's size sets its shape. The embed button offers three:

| Shape | Width | Height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square | 400 | 400 |
| Landscape | 400 | 300 |
| Portrait | 340 | 604 |

Without the chart, the card is shorter. The embed button on outcome.xyz gives you the exact size.

## Before you go live

- Links in the embed carry no `utm` parameters, so outcome.xyz can't attribute those visitors to you. For attributed links, see [Showcase markets](/sdk/guides/recipes/showcase-markets).
- Outcome isn't available in some jurisdictions. See [Risks](/risks#geographic-restrictions).
